About PlantCare AI

PlantCare AI is an intelligent plant disease detection system that combines Deep Learning and Gemini AI to identify diseases from plant leaf images. It provides farmers and gardeners with accurate disease predictions along with symptoms, causes, treatment recommendations, and preventive measures in seconds.
